# Quick Start - Contact Form 🚀

## ✅ Integration Complete!

Your contact form is now connected to your backend API:
**`https://nyanopan.onrender.com/api/v1/contact-us/`**

## Test It Now (3 Steps)

### 1️⃣ Open the Contact Page
Navigate to: **`http://localhost:3000/contact`**

### 2️⃣ Fill Out the Form
- **Name:** Your name
- **Email:** your@email.com
- **Phone:** (optional)
- **Subject:** Choose from dropdown
- **Message:** Type your message

### 3️⃣ Click "Send Message"
- Watch the loading spinner
- See the success message
- Form clears automatically

## That's It! 🎉

Your contact form submissions now go directly to your backend at Render.com.

## Where to Find It

📍 **URL:** `/contact`  
📍 **Navigation:** Footer → Information → "Contact Us"

## What Happens When Someone Submits?

```
User submits form
     ↓
Validated on frontend
     ↓
Sent to your backend API
     ↓
Your backend processes it
     ↓
Success message shown to user
```

## Check Backend Logs

Visit your Render.com dashboard to see incoming submissions in real-time!
